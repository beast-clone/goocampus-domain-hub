The Case for Structured Training

Why Should You Consider These Programmes?

Seven reasons doctors choose structured, hospital-based clinical training.

Hospital-Based Clinical Exposure

Gain structured clinical exposure within established hospital environments and develop practical skills and understanding in your chosen clinical area.

Focused Clinical Training

Choose a programme aligned with your area of interest and gain specialised experience through a structured clinical training environment.

Stipend During Training

Eligible programmes offer a monthly stipend, allowing you to gain clinical experience while receiving financial support during your training.

Course Completion Certification

Upon successful completion of the programme requirements, candidates will receive a Course Completion Certificate jointly awarded by Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited and Apollo Hospitals Educational & Research Foundation (AHERF).

Multiple Programme Locations

Explore opportunities across multiple cities and hospital locations, with programme availability varying by speciality and location.

Strengthen Your Career Profile

Develop specialised clinical experience, build professional networks and gain exposure to an established healthcare environment — helping you take a more focused next step in your medical career.

Build a Stronger Professional Portfolio

Go beyond clinical exposure. Depending on the programme and opportunities available, gain exposure to research, clinical audits, academic activities, presentations and other portfolio-building initiatives that can support your long-term career development.

Before You Enquire

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the Fellowship Programmes for?

The Fellowship Programmes are intended for PG-qualified doctors seeking advanced, focused clinical training.

Who are the Post-MBBS Certification Programmes for?

These programmes are intended for doctors who have completed MBBS and are looking to develop focused clinical skills through structured training.

What is the fee for the Fellowship Programmes?

The listed course fee is ₹10,00,000 + 18% GST.

What is the fee for the Post-MBBS Certification Programmes?

The listed course fee is ₹3,00,000 + 18% GST.

Is a stipend provided?

Yes. The Fellowship Programmes provide a monthly stipend of ₹1,00,000.

For Post-MBBS Certification Programmes, the stipend ranges from ₹45,000 to ₹65,000 per month, depending on the hospital location.

Where are the programmes conducted?

Programmes are available across multiple hospital locations in India. Programme and location availability varies, so please contact GooCampusWorld for current availability.

Who awards the certificate?

Upon successful completion, candidates will receive a Course Completion Certificate jointly awarded by Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited and Apollo Hospitals Educational & Research Foundation (AHERF).

Can GooCampusWorld help me choose the right programme?

Yes. GooCampusWorld can help you understand the available options based on your qualification, clinical interests and career objectives.

Are seats guaranteed after submitting an enquiry?

No. Programme admission and selection are subject to the applicable eligibility criteria, selection process and seat availability.

GooCampusWorld provides information, guidance and application support for the programmes listed on this page. Programme admission and selection are subject to the applicable eligibility criteria, selection process and seat availability of the respective institution. Programme details, fees, stipend, locations and availability are as shared by the programme partner and are subject to change.
