Why the Gulf

Why More Doctors Are Choosing the Gulf

Doctors across India are building their careers in the Gulf for good reasons. Tax-free income, world-class hospitals, a structured licensing pathway, international exposure and a high quality of life make the region one of the most sought-after destinations for medical professionals.

But every one of these opportunities begins at the same point. You need a valid medical license, and that starts with clearing the licensing exam. This batch is built to help you prepare for exactly that.

    Who is this batch for?

    GPs, Dentists and Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Unani practitioners who meet the eligibility listed for their profession. All of the points listed are mandatory.

    What does the batch cover?

    Preparation for the Gulf licensing exams through weekly live classes, study materials, MCQs, the latest recalls, mock tests and live Q&A.

    Which authorities does the batch train for?

    DHA, DOH, SHA, MOHAP, SCFHS, QCHP, NHRA, Kuwait MOH and Oman MOH. You can pick any one of them, based on the emirate or country where you want to practise.
